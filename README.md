# 📊 Sales Dashboard Overview (Tableau)

An interactive Tableau dashboard that tracks sales performance, profit and quantity sold, with a year-over-year comparison and a breakdown by state, customer segment and region.

## 🎯 Objective
To show how sales, profit and quantity changed from 2021 to 2022, which states and regions perform best, and which customer segments drive the most sales.

## 📌 Key Metrics (2022 vs 2021)
| Metric | Value | Change vs 2021 |
|---|---|---|
| Total Sales | $733K | ▲ 20.36% |
| Total Profit | $93K | ▲ $0.14 |
| Total Quantity | 12K | ▲ 26.83% |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Sales grew 20.36% and quantity sold grew 26.83% compared with the previous year.
- Consumer is the largest segment, with a monthly sales peak of 50.23K. Corporate peaks at 44.64K and Home Office at 29.71K.
- By region, East leads with 213K in sales, followed by West (250K), Central (147K) and South (123K). _Check this order against your dashboard before you publish._
- Sadie Pawthorne manages the highest sales (250,128), followed by Chuck Magee (213,083).
- Two state maps show which states are above and below the U.S. average for sales and for profit.

## 🧩 Dashboard Features
- **KPI cards with sparklines:** total sales, profit and quantity, with year-over-year change
- **Symbol map:** sales and profit distribution by state
- **Area charts:** monthly sales for each customer segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- **State maps:** states above and below the U.S. sales and profit average
- **Bar chart:** total sales by region and manager
- **Measure switcher:** changes the segment chart between sales and other measures
